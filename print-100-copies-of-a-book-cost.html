Cost to Print 100 Copies of a Book

Independent book publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: 100 copies of a 200-page paperback typically costs $1,200–$2,200 total — around $12–$22 per copy including setup. Good for authors testing a market.

Setup vs per-copy cost

Setup (plates, layout, proof) is fixed; per-copy cost is variable. At low quantities setup dominates, which is why per-copy prices fall as runs grow.
