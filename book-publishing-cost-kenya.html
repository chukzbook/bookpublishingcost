What pushes the cost up

  1. Page count. Packages are priced to a page basis — paper, printing and layout all scale with length.
  2. Colour interior. A handful of colour pages changes the printing method, and is usually the single biggest jump.
  3. Print run. Small runs print digitally; larger runs print offset, where the unit cost drops sharply. More copies can cost less per book.
  4. Extras. Editing, marketing campaigns, press releases, author websites and launch events are add-ons, priced by scope.

Editing is the one most authors under-budget. A 90,000-word manuscript needing developmental work is a different project from one that needs a proofread.

Frequently asked

How much does it cost to publish a book in Kenya?

Between KSh 90,500 and KSh 1,295,000 depending on the package. Most first-time authors publishing a standard-length book land between KSh 168,000 and KSh 323,500.

Can I publish for less?

Not with a full service. At the lower end you are doing the editing, cover and layout yourself, and paying mainly for ISBN and printing.

Do I need to buy copies?

Printing is included in the packages. Distribution to bookstores and online retailers is separate, and can be print-on-demand with no upfront stock, or a print run.

How long does it take?

Typically 6 to 8 weeks from the final manuscript for a standard package. Rush timelines are possible and are priced as a rush.

Do I keep the rights?

Yes. You keep your copyright and 100% of your royalties. We are a self-publishing company — not a traditional publisher, and not a partnership.

What do you need to give an exact figure in KES?

Your manuscript in Word format, the approximate page count, whether you need colour inside, and how many copies you want.
