Cost to Print 50 Copies of a Book

Independent publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: Printing 50 copies is the smallest economical offset run: expect $800–$1,500 total for a standard 200-page book, because setup dominates at this quantity. Per-copy cost is highest here.

Why per-copy price falls with quantity

Setup is fixed; paper and binding scale. Doubling the run roughly halves the per-copy cost until setup stops mattering.
