What drives the cost of publishing a book?

Six cost centers decide your total. Editing is usually the largest service cost; printing depends on quantity and color; marketing is optional but increasingly essential.

  • Editing — $500–$2,000 for a standard manuscript
  • Cover design — $300–$1,500 for professional work
  • Interior formatting — $150–$800 depending on complexity
  • ISBN & barcode — $125 per ISBN from Bowker
  • Printing — $2.50–$8 per paperback copy (100–500 copies)
  • Marketing — $500–$5,000+ depending on ambition
