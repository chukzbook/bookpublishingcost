What drives the cost of publishing a book?

  • Editing — the largest service cost for most books
  • Cover design — professional design matters for sales
  • Interior formatting — layout, fonts, and image handling
  • ISBN & barcode — required for retail distribution
  • Printing — quantity and color determine per-copy cost
  • Marketing — optional but increasingly essential
