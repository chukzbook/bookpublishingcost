Cost to Publish a Business Book

Independent book publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: Business books cost $2,500–$8,000 — authors often add professional editing ($1,500–$3,000) and larger print runs for events and speaking.

Cost breakdown

Editing is the largest variable; full-color interiors and illustration drive print costs up. Bundled publishing services often cost less than hiring each provider separately.
