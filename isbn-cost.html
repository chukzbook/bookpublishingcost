How Much Does ISBN & Barcode Cost?

Independent book publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: A single ISBN costs $125 from Bowker (the US ISBN agency); a block of 10 costs $295. Barcodes are typically included or cost $10–$25.

What affects isbn & barcode cost?

Page count, complexity, and provider experience are the main drivers. Always ask for a bundled quote that includes revisions.
