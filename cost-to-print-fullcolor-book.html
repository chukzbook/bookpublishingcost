Cost to Print a Full-Color Printing Book

Independent book publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: Full-color interior printing costs $0.08–$0.15 per page at print-on-demand, or roughly 3–5× the cost of black-and-white for offset runs. A 100-page full-color book costs $1,200–$2,500 to print at 500 copies.

Full-Color Printing printing: what to know

Setup fees are paid once per print run. Larger runs lower the per-copy price. Print-on-demand has no setup but a higher per-copy cost.
