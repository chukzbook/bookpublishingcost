How Much Does Cover Design Cost?

Independent book publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: A professional book cover design costs $300–$1,500 depending on the designer's experience. Premium designers with publishing-industry credits charge $500–$2,500. A basic template cover can cost as little as $50–$150.

What affects cover design cost?

Page count, complexity, and provider experience are the main drivers. Always ask for a bundled quote that includes revisions.
