Cost to Print a Hardcover Book

Independent book publishing cost guide · Updated for 2026

Short answer: Hardcover printing costs $8–$20 per copy depending on quantity, page count, and case style. Setup is higher than paperback, so per-unit cost drops sharply as quantity rises.

Hardcover printing: what to know

Setup fees are paid once per print run. Larger runs lower the per-copy price. Print-on-demand has no setup but a higher per-copy cost.
